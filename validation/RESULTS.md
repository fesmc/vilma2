Pass 2 agrees with pass 1 to the third printed digit (V2 − V1 rms at 21 ka
5.138 against 5.141 m without the toroidal field), so the comparison with 907bc74
below holds for both.

**The production 3-D row is V7** (since c92d70a): `D-V2-3D.l170.tor`, the toroidal
field of the 3-D elements on, the model default since visc-shift 0d1eac9.
`D-V2-3D.l170.fe` (toroidal off, the default when it was run) is its control. The
1Db runs have no laterally 3-D element (`visc3d split: 0 of 220`), so `l_toroidal`
has nothing to act on there; the V1 runs are unchanged. The run ids are named once,
in `analysis/Published.jl`.

V2 − V1 with the 3-D viscosity (`tables/v2v1.md`): rms 2.60 m at 21 ka on the grid,
2.18 m area-weighted (5.14 and 4.08 m without the toroidal field), at most 0.7 % of
the signal; 0.096 mm/yr in present-day uplift rate (0.183). The degree-2, order-1
part at 21 ka is 0.49 m in 3-D and 0.37 m in 1Db, against 7.74 m and 7.86 m at
907bc74: the rotation fixes of 304f4cb removed that pattern. What remains in 3-D has
no dominant low-degree part: the largest part up to degree 8 is degree 5, order 2
at 0.35 m. 1Db: 0.63 m at 21 ka.

The toroidal field itself (`tables/toroidal.md`) changes relative sea level by up to
92 m (2.1 m rms) at 10 ka and present-day ocean-mean rsl by −0.06 m, leaves the
budget closure at 102.8 %, and costs ×1.47 in wall time (2.67 against 1.82 min per
kyr). Map: `deglac-toroidal-10ka.png`.

### Against the reference results (paper repository, model 907bc74)

- Block A and the A-i/B-i rows of block B: unchanged.
- LGM reference: ocean-mean rsl −101.25 m (−101.89 m at 907bc74, −101.85 m at
  304f4cb). Cause: the forcing remap. Before 40a94db it put 0.66 % too much ice
  on the Gauss grid: area-mean 26 ka ice thickness 138.10 m in the 304f4cb
  reference, against 137.19 m now and 137.188 m in the GLAC-1D source grid.
  The remap now conserves the ice.
- Deglaciation budget (`tables/blockD.md`): delivered/target 102.9 % (3-D) and
  103.2 % (1Db), against 102.6 % and 102.9 %; target 94.21 m (94.98 m).
- VILMA2 − VILMA1 (`tables/v2v1.md`), rms of rsl at 21 ka: 3-D 5.14 m (9.23 m),
  1Db 0.63 m (7.66 m); ocean-mean rsl at present day differs by 0.35 m (0.86 m)
  and 0.23 m (0.72 m); present-day uplift rate rms 0.18 mm/yr (0.30) and
  0.030 mm/yr (0.23). Between the two sets lie the rotation fixes of 304f4cb
  (sign of the centrifugal potential, relaxation viscosity, in-step coupling),
  which the paper log had identified as the degree-2 order-1 part of the residual.
- 3-D minus 1Db (lateral-viscosity signal): 17.25 m rms at 21 ka (17.37 m).
